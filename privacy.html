Privacy

Your privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

In short: this site has no cookies, no analytics or tracking, no adverts, no sign-up and no forms. I do not collect personal information through it. Everything on the site, including the fonts, is served from the site itself.

Who I am

grantanthony.co.uk is my personal photography website. I am Grant C Anthony, based in Scotland, and I am responsible for this site. You can contact me at gcanth@gmail.com.

When you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes. I do not have access to these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Settings saved in your browser. If you use the "On the wall" preview, your choice of room, wall colour and frame is saved in your own browser so it is the same next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to me. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

The photographs

Photographs are re-saved when I upload them, which removes the location and other information embedded in the original camera files. The camera settings shown beside each photograph are read from the file at upload; location data is never read.

Some photographs show people in public places. If you appear in one and would like it removed, email me and I will take it down.

If you email me

I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. I use your message only to reply to you. I do not share it, sell it or add you to any mailing list.

Links to other sites

Links to Instagram take you to a site run by Meta, which has its own privacy policy. This site does not embed Instagram content or share anything with Instagram when you visit.

Questions

If you have a question about anything on this page, or want something removed, email me.

Changes

If the site changes in a way that affects this page, I will update it and the date at the top.
