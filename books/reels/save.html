How to post it
  1. Tap Save video, then Save Video in the menu that opens. It goes into Photos.
  2. Tap Copy caption.
  3. In Instagram, switch to the account above, tap +, then Reel, and pick the video.
  4. Add your music, tap Next, paste the caption, and use Edit cover to pick a frame near the end.
  5. Share it, then close the GitHub issue.
