Reels to post

Each Reel is silent so you can add a trending sound in Instagram. Tick one off when it's up; the tick is remembered on this phone.

How to post one
  1. Tap Download video. On an iPhone, open it from Downloads in the Files app, tap Share, then Save Video.
  2. Tap Copy caption.
  3. In Instagram, switch to the account shown, tap +, then Reel, and pick the video.
  4. Tap the music note, choose a sound from Trending, and pick the part of the song you want.
  5. Tap Next, paste the caption, and Share.
