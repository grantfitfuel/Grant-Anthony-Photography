In short: every photograph on this site is mine, and you are welcome to share a link to it. Please ask before using a photograph anywhere else.

Who owns them

All photographs on grantanthony.co.uk are © Grant C Anthony, all rights reserved. In the UK, copyright in a photograph belongs to the photographer automatically, with no need to register it. The lessons and their text are mine too.

What you can do

  • Share a link to the site, to a photograph or to a lesson
  • Print a lesson's cheat sheet for your own use

Please ask first before you

  • Download a photograph to use it anywhere else
  • Post it on your own website or social media
  • Print it, publish it, or use it in anything sold or advertised
  • Use it to train or test any AI system

How to ask

Email me at gcanth@gmail.com and tell me which photograph (its title is shown when you open it), what you would like to use it for, and where it will appear. If you are interested in a print, the same address works; there is an "Ask about a print" link under each photograph.

Other people's work

Some photographs show street art, signs or buildings designed by other people. Those works remain theirs. My copyright covers the photograph, not the artwork in it.
