The Alchemy of Autumn

A longer lens pulls one small patch of woodland out of the whole. Low light catches the yellow birch leaves at the front so they glow, while the silver trunks and tangled branches behind stay cool and shaded. Bracken turning rust at the bottom of the frame adds the last of the colour.
