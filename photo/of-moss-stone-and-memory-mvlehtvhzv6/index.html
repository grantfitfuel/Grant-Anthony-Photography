Photograph · The Hermitage, Perthshire

Of Moss, Stone and Memory

Wet boulders thick with moss lead down to a dark, quiet stretch of river. Beyond them an old stone bridge sits half hidden among copper and gold trees, and a soft mist hangs over everything. Fallen leaves are scattered across the rocks in the foreground, the first sign of the season turning.
