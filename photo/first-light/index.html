Photograph · Deadvlei, Namibia

First light

Deadvlei, Namibia, as the first sun reaches the pan. At 400 mm the dune behind becomes a plain, dark backdrop, still in shadow, so the lit trunk and branches of a single dead camel thorn tree stand out against it. There’s no horizon and no sky, just the tree and the light on it.
